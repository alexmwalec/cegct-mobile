# CEGCT - Civil Engineering & Governance Citizen Tracking App

CEGCT is an Android application designed for citizen engagement, civil infrastructure reporting, issue tracking, and communication with local government officials in Malawi.

## Features
- **Authentication & Onboarding:** Secure registration, email verification, login, password recovery, and anonymous login.
- **Problem Reporting:** Category selection, photo capture, GPS location pinning, description, and review.
- **My Reports & Case Tracking:** Summary dashboard, filter options, offline sync banner, timeline views, officer interaction, rating, and case reopening.
- **Interactive Map & Chat:** Nearby reports visualization and direct messaging with assigned inspectors/officers.
- **Notifications & Inspection:** Push notifications (FCM) and inspection management.
- **Localization:** Full support for English and Chichewa (`values-ny`).

## Tech Stack
- Kotlin & Jetpack Compose (Material 3)
- Coroutines & Flow
- Architecture: MVVM + Clean Architecture principles
- Gradle (Kotlin DSL) & Version Catalog
